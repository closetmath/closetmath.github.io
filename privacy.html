Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This policy covers Closet Math Uploader, the tool we use to publish videos to the Closet Math YouTube channel.

Who uses it

Only the owner of the Closet Math channel. It isn't available to the public and nobody else can sign in to it.

What it accesses

With the channel owner's permission, it uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos, set thumbnails and add captions on the Closet Math channel. It doesn't read, collect or store data about viewers or any other YouTube users.

What it stores

It stores one sign-in token for the Closet Math channel on our own server, so it can publish on schedule. The token is never shared or sold. The owner can revoke it at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google and YouTube

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's privacy policy is at policies.google.com/privacy.

Contact

Questions: the contact email listed on the Closet Math YouTube channel.
